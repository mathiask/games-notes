Notes on Empire of the Sun
==========================

DRMs
----
* Intelligence (and SR) rolls with movement into/in/out of ZOI: -2

Easily Forgotten Rules
----------------------
see also: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3373960/rules-that-catch-players-by-surprise-in-empire-of
### Replacements
* send to Europe roll has a -1 DRM for ISR
* B29 entering as replacement cannot do strategic bombing this turn

### Activation
* Activation paths are only blocked by:
** *water hexsides" of ZOIs
** *land hexsides* of hexes with only enemy ground/air units

### Movement
* Strategic Air Movement possible as reinforcement on the next turn (8.33)
* Air movement across friendly airfields, even if they have enemy ground
  unit. Move must not end in a hex with enemy ground units.
* barges: only 1x for 3OC offensive (not reaction)
** ground movement captures empty hexes, AA movement *doesn't*

### Battles
* SR roll: no card but DRM-2 for ZOI (OC used if surprise no event without EC)
** no SR roll for hex if only ground movement
* Reaction uses (logistics value | OC) + efficiency, activated units must participate
  in a battle
* Enemy units in battle hexes do *not* block activation path
* Support ships (non CV/air stacked with CV outside of BH) add soak, but not attack
** damage is *not* limited by air/CV of opponent
* Japanese can transfer one (non-critical) CV* step loss
* Critical hits also apply when AN attacks only ground (both players)
** if hit < min(step), reaction player picks among the minimal steps
* Only the last ground step is safe from air/naval; that might be the intrinsic
  step in Japan and China
** reaction player decides last step unit, but intrinsic defence is always the last step
* Reaction player wins AN combat if only they have air left
* +3 DRM against AA only if units (or HQ) in the battle hex before reaction
** offensive AA units always have half defensive strength
* Offensive players decides retreat hex (no enemy, no over-stack, not where the attack came from; friendly named location if possible)
+ PBM only for non-ground units that did not use strategic movement

### Political Will
* political will +3 if JP resources <= 3 in turns 5 - 12 (1x/game)
* political will +1 for successful strategic bombing

### Stacking
* no limit during offensive (before PBM)
* air units are removed before ground
* naval in supply return the next turn, air and ground one turn later(?) (OOS => eliminate)
** cannot be delayed
