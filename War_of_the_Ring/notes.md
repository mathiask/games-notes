Notes on Rules Details and Exceptions in War of the Ring
========================================================

General
-------
* phase <: turn (no "rounds")

Revealing
---------
* Cities and strongholds heal and activate
** not possible when revealed by hunt
* !!!NEW!!! *can* enter, reveal in, leave sieged strongholds

Hunt Allocation / Hunt
----------------------
* allocation: dice <= max(1, #companions in fellowship)
* 6 is a hit; FP die in box => +1 modifier (incl. re-rolls)
** re-rolls for #Nazgul > 0, army, stronghold
** eye damage = number of hits rolled
** extra draw due to stronghold(s) (from, through, to)
*** eye drawn => 0 damage
* reveals on tiles drawn from events don't count in FP city/ctronghold
* damage: guide ability of new guide may be used immediately
* --
* /Mordor/
** put *eye tiles* back + extra tiles from played cards
** no dies rolled, but tile drawn for every step
*** eye damage = number of dice (SP+FP) in box
** FAQ: Cancelling "Shelob" (Sting) must be done before rolling d6

Movement
--------
* 2 armies move, or one attack; no piece can move twice
* moving through an (empty) enemy settlement captures it
* Nazgul cannot move into FP stronghold, FP lead must stop @SP stronghold
** neither can enter a besieged stronghold
** stop also applies when separating while fellowship is in a SP stronghold
* stacking limit is checked at the end of (multi-step) movement

Battle
------
* combat cards, low priority first, defenders break ties
* roll before main fight: no re-rolls, opponent modifiers
* characters' leadership is their leadership, leaders=1
* fortifications (&cities): first round only
* retreat into stronghold: possible before *every* combat round
** stacking limit 5, surplus units become reinforcements
* cease attack; if not, then option to retreat
* when retreating units from *non active* nation can cross borders
** but must leave on next movement
** cannot retreat into region with enemy settlement (not "free")

Politics
--------
* events triggering battle, 3 Ent cards, Dead Men of Dunharrow affect potitics
** others don't

Misc
----
* Hand limit 6, immediate discard down
* no hide|move in Mordor => 1 corruption
* 1 Elven Ring / turn max (both sides; no WotW)
* Action Tokens (from Hunt for the Ring; used also in online play)
  * Shadow:
    - Move Nazgul and Minions
    - Advance a Shadow Nation on the Political Track
  * Free Peoples:
    - Draw one Event card
    - Advance a Free Peoples Nation on the Political Track
