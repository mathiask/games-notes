[Geoff Speare, @galfridus, Posted,Oct 10 (edited)]
The next printing of HF4A (submitted on or around October 2021) has updated rules and cards. This is my unofficial list of the changes.

I went carefully through all the rules and noted anything which was more than a clarification. If you find something I missed, let me know.

Major Core/M1/M2 rules changes:
• None. If you don't use these changes, your game experience is pretty much the same.

Significant Core rules changes:
• Numerous examples corrected!
• Colony domes are not bit limited, but you only score for those supplied [B8 tip]
• Factory-assist liftoff into a Hazard ignores that Hazard [H6c]
• Factory-assist landing/liftoff is not a Hazard for Colony Sites [H6c]
• Air-eater refuel grants 5 tanks minus Fuel Consumption (changed from 5) [I5c]
• Glitch event only affects stacks without Colocated Humans [K2b]
• Martial law applies at the start of a fundraise rather than the end [O2b]
• 1st player does not change at end of cycle if in martial law [O5d]
• Anchoring GEO or Lofstrom Loop Bernal in Home Orbit requires Epic Hazard Roll [Glossary, Epic Hazard Roll]
• War of Independence changed [Glossary]
○ War ends at end of current season
○ Specific effects are different (see Glossary)

Significant Module 1 & 2 rules changes
• Numerous Futures clarifications (see below)
• "Factory Load Only" freighters cannot be loaded at Home Bernals [1B3b]
• GEO Elevator persists if GEO Bernal unanchored; any other Bernal anchored there can use it to boost for 1x [1B9f]
• Each named Future can only be completed once per game [1D1a]
• Endgame Futures require that the Future card be operational and Colocated with a Human at game end [1D2b]
• Gaining a delegate during exomigration is optional [2A6c]
• Placing 2nd Bernal at Home Bernal costs 10 Aqua [2A7c]
• Bernals can activate other dirt thrusters when moving [2B2c]
• Climate Control Bernal must be Anchored at L1 to receive Home Ability [2B4c]
• Miner Colonists give an extra refuel during a refuel op rather than a free refuel [2C1a]
• Engineer Colonists can be at the Factory or Anchored Bernal to give extra ET Production [2C1d]
• On-board nuclear supports on Colonists can be used for nanofacturing [2C3]
• Biomechs ability does not work during war/anarchy [2C4j]
• Iceworms no longer make Epic Hazard Operations a free action

Other Core rules changes:
• "Bank" defined [Glossary]
• You can cargo transfer Fuel between Rocket/Bernal Stacks as FTs or on the Fuel Strip. However, a Crew dirt thruster is limited to accept one Fuel Tank per Turn. [G1c]
• Placing a delegate when building a Colony is optional [G3c]
• Phileas Fogg cannot use Bernal cards [G2c]
• Load Glory Chit: "Site never visited before by Humans" removed [G5, L]
• Free marketing a card with no Factories nets 10 Aqua [I3b]
• Project Valkyrie clarified to only affect its own Rocket Stack [J1c, tip]
• Clarification of what "or" means in reactor requirements [J1d, tip]
• You may choose to make a card non-operational [J3e]
• Negotiation: Anchored Bernals and promoted cards cannot be transferred [N]
• Crew rescue can also be used on Colonists [N3d]
• Colonist specialties may be shared [N7]
• Any change in delegates (add, move, remove) causes a vote tally [O3]
• Freedom to Roam allows use of Space Elevators [O5f]
• You cannot use your big cube for the Sunspot cube [O6b]
• Bank defined as LEO/Home Bernal [Glossary]
• Cards at anchored Bernals immune to CME [Glossary]
• Glitch Roll affects all your Colocated Stacks [Glossary]
• Glitch trigger does not happen when ET Producing to outpost [Glossary]
• Murder/suicide of Colonist does not remove a delegate; the Colonist returns to Bank [Glossary]

Other Module 1 & 2 rules changes:
• You cannot abandon a Dirtside Factory if it would leave a Bernal without a Dirtside [1B6c]
• You may choose to Decommission your Promoted Freighter instead of cube swapping [1B7a]
• Move-On rule removed (anchoring a Bernal in same space) [2A5h in old rules]
• Bernals not being used for movement must convert fuel to FTs [2B2d]
• UN receives 2 Aqua when Home Bernal Anchored [2B3b]
• All Anchored Bernals protect from events [2A5]

Futures Changes/Clarifications
• TW FUTURE CHANGES/CLARIFICATIONS [1D3]
○ Spacefaring: TW Thruster must be at Bernal with 8+ Dirtside Hydration
○ Mini-Black Hole: TW Thruster must be at centaur; 10 isotope must be spent
○ Mass Beam: TW Thruster must be at Promoted Bernal; effect grants freighters 3 thrust
○ Fusion Candle: TW Thruster must be at Promoted Bernal
○ Protium Fusion: TW Thruster must be at Promoted Bernal
○ Enzmann Starship: TW Thruster must be with ad astra Stack
○ Lithiated Ammonia Ice Starship: TW Thruster must be with ad astra Stack
• FREIGHTER FUTURE CHANGES/CLARIFICATIONS [1D4]
○ Terraform: Freighter must be at Promoted Bernal
○ Beehive Ark: Freighter must be at Promoted Bernal
○ Exoplanet: Freighter must be at Sedna
○ Beanstalk: Freighter (and Human) must be at site connected to constructed Space Elevator. +3 VP for each elevator connected to a Factory (of any player).
○ Golden Apples: (no change)
○ Antimatter: Freighter must be at Promoted Bernal
○ Star Wisp: Freighter must be at Sunlens
• COLONIST FUTURE CHANGES/CLARIFICATIONS [1D5]
○ Colonist must be at the named location of the Future or at specified Bernal, if any. (These will be spelled out below, but this is a general rule.)
○ New Venus: Colonist must be on comet. Venus and Comet immediately have all tokens removed and are Busted. No longer Endgame.
○ Supreme Cult: No location. No longer Endgame, but you must control this Colonist at game end to receive the VP.
○ Artificial Consciousness: Colonist must be at Astrobiology Dirtside.
○ SETI: Colonist must be at one of the trojans.
○ Secession: Colonist must be at Promoted Anchored Bernal. Both Colonists have the same Secession Future text.
○ Footfall: Colonist must be at comet. Comet immediately has all tokens removed and is Busted. No longer Endgame.
○ ET Life: Colonist must be at Astrobiological Colony.
○ Aerostat: Colonist must be at Promoted Bernal.
○ Pan Sapiens: Colonist must be at Factory connected to Space Elevator.
○ Dyson Bubble: Colonist must be at Mercury Site. Endgame.
○ Submariner: Colonist must be at Submarine Factory or Colony.
○ Beanstalk: Colonist must be at Site connected to constructed Space Elevator. +3 VP for each elevator connected to a Factory (of any player).
○ TNO: Colonist must be at Neptune Factory.
○ Uplift: Colonist must be at Promoted Bernal.

APPENDIX:
• V1 Quick Start
○ Player count changed to 2+, competitive.
• V2 Race to Saturn
○ Game end requires landing on Titan surface (not aerostat).
• V4 Altruism
○ Module 0 can be used.
○ Patent Deck Setup: Remove half of each patent deck sight unseen.
○ Research Auction Operation:
§ Take a card, plus supports, for 1 Aqua per card taken.
§ Marketeer faction privilege: 3 cards costs 2 Aqua.
§ Academia hand limit applies, even in solo games.
• V5 Hermes Fall
○ Patent Deck setup as in V4, but Mass Driver is always added to thruster deck.
○ Research Auction Operation as in V4.
• V6 CEO
○ Patent Deck Setup as in V4.
○ Research Auction Operation as in V4.
○ Faction privilege rules as in V4 (instead of special rules for this scenario)
• V7 Bios: Earth
○ VP change: victory chits from previous games plus 1/2 HF score is your final score.
• V8 Bios: Venus/Mars
○ Place Earth overlay if starting on Mars.
○ Martians cannot get glory chits from Mars moonlets.
○ Venus and Mars LEO-equivalents do not provide solar flare protection [V8c].
○ Campaign transition rules changed - source of winning macroorganism determines starting world [V8f]
• V9 Sirens
○ When splitting decks, Sirens get the extra card (if any).
○ Quick Start: all players draw from one deck during first cycle, then split decks.
○ Lofstrom Loop Bernal can also be used to build Uranus Space Elevator.
