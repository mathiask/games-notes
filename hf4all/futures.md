General
=======
* Each named Future can only be completed once per game [1D1a]
* Endgame Futures require that the Future card be operational and Colocated
  with a Human at game end [1D2b]


TW Thrusters
============

* Req: Ad astra exit with 2 promoted Colonists and a Mobile Factory
** *Future has to be at the ad astra exit (PNP)*
* VP: 12

### Fusion Candle Future
* Req: Triton Colony & Promoted Bernal with Neptune Aerostat Dirtside

* VP: 14

### Lithiated Ammonia Ice Starship Future
* Req: Ad astra exit with 10 isotope fuel
** *Future has to be at the ad astra exit*
* VP: 14

### Mass Beam Future

* Eff: Your Powersat adds +2 thrust
* VP: 7

### Mini-black Hole Future

** *Future has to be at the centaur (PNP)*
* Eff: double all isotope refuel

### Protium Fusion Future
* Req: Promoted Bernal with H Dirtside

## 1D3. List of TW Thruster Futures
(see https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2609092/futures-lists-re-write)
a. Spacefaring Future: Req = Zubrim-GDM Thruster and your human at a single
    Bernal with 8+ dirtside hydration. Ongoing Effect: You are allowed 1 extra
    Colonist. Immediately earn 7 VP.
b. Mini-black Hole Future: Req = Amat-initiated H-B Magnetic-inertial Thruster
    and your human at an industrialized centaur with 10 isotope FTs. Return FTs to
    pool. Ongoing Effect: Double your isotope refuel. Immediately earn 10 VP.
c. Mass Beam Future: Req = Dusty Plasma Thruster and your human at promoted
    Bernal with Io or Triton Dirtside. Ongoing Effect: Your Powersat adds +2
    thrust instead of +1 thrust, and gives Freighters a net thrust of 3. No effect
    if you do not have the Powersat ability. Immediately earn 7 VP.
d. Fusion Candle Future: Req = Triton Colony and Promoted Bernal with Neptune
    Aerostat Dirtside with Daedalus 3HE-D Inertial Fusion Thruster and your human
    at either location. Ongoing Effect: Double your isotope refuel. Immediately
    earn 14 VP.
e. Protium Fusion Future: Req = Crossfire H-B Focus Fusion Thruster and your
    human at a Promoted Bernal with H Dirtside. Ongoing Effect: Double your
    isotope refuel. Immediately earn 10 VP.
f. Enzmann Starship Future: Req = Colliding FRC 3He-D Fusion Thruster, your
    two promoted Colonists, and your Mobile Factory at Ad astra exit. Decommission
    entire stack at Ad astra exit. Effect: Immediately earn 12 VP.
g. Lithiated Ammonia Ice Starship Future: Req = Solem Medusa Tugged Orion
    Thruster and your human at Ad astra exit with 10 isotope fuel. Decommission
    entire stack at Ad astra exit. Effect: Immediately earn 14 VP.

### Beehive Ark Future

* Req: Claim Sedna

* Req: Industrialize kreutz sungrazer

* Req: A Mobile Factory at either the neutrino or EM sunlens

* Req: Freighter at Promoted Bernal at a non-martian Atmospheric Dirtside

* **Delta: Freighter has to be at the space**


## 1D4. List of Freighter Futures
a. Terraform Future: Req = Archimedes Palmer Lens Freighter and your human at
    Promoted Bernal at a non-Martian Atmospheric Dirtside. Effect: Immediately
    earn 8 VP.
b. Beehive Ark Future: Req = KEST Hoop Dirt Launcher Freighter and your human
    at Promoted Bernal anchored at a Synodic Comet. Effect: Immediately earn 7
    VP.
c. Exoplanet Hunt Future: Req = Fission GCR Freighter and your human at Sedna
    with your claim. Endgame: Earn 12 VP if you maintain a claim on Sedna
    (Freighter and human are not required to be present).
d. Beanstalk Future: Req = Have 3+ Space Elevators built by any player, with
    your human and KESTS Hoop Dirt Launcher Freighter co-located with one of
    them. Only 1 player is allowed to complete a Beanstalk future. Endgame:
    You earn 3 VP for each Factory (of any player) connected to a Space
    Elevator.
e. Golden Apples Future: Req = Have D-nanotube Dirt Launcher Freighter and
    your human present at the industrialized kreutz sungrazer. Note that the
    Mobile Factory and the Human on kreutz are allowed to escape before
    vaporization at the end of the yellow season. Before they leave, it is
    assumed to have produced instrumentation to measure Sol during its close
    pass. Ongoing Effect: You ignore solar flares. Immediately earn 14 VP.
f. Antimatter Future: Req = Z-Pinch 3He-D Target Fusion Freighter and your
    human at a Promoted Bernal with S Dirtside. Ongoing Effect: Double your
    isotope refuel. Immediately earn 10 VP.
g. Star Wisp Future: Req = Endgame: Magnetic Mirror Beam Rider Freighter, your
    Mobile Factory, and your human at either the neutrino sunlens or the EM
    sunlens. Neutrino sunlens: 6 VP, EM sunlens: 11 VP.


Colonists
=========
Unless otherwise specified, the Colonist does not have to be at the named
location to complete the Future.

### Aerostat Future
* Req: Promoted Bernal with Aerostat Dirtside
* Eff: As a permanent Ability, can homestead as a free action
* VP: 14

### Artificial Consciousness Future
* Req: 2 promoted Colonists at an Astrobiology Dirtside
* Eff: May free market any number of cards
* VP: 10

### Beanstalk Future (only once)
* Req: 3+ Space Elevators built by any player
* VP (endgame): +3 VP for each Factory (of any player) connected to a Space
  Elevator
* **Delta: VP per elevator not per factory**

### Dyson Bubble Future
* Req: Both Sites of Mercury industrialized by any player, colonist at Mercury site.
* VP (Endgame): + 5 VP per Factory owned on Mercury

### ET Life Future
* Req: Have 2 or more Astrobiological Colonies
* VP (endgame): +2 VP per Astrobiological Colony

### Footfall Future
* Req: Decommission an operational thruster and its supports with net thrust
  7+ on an Industrialized Synodic Comet of yours, busting the site and
  removing the claim and factory.
* Eff: **Casus Belli**
* VP: 10
* **Delta: Busts the site removing claim and factory, no endgame**

### New Venus Future
* Req: Decommission an operational thruster and its supports with net thrust
  7+ on an Industrialized Synodic Comet of yours, busting the site and
  removing the claim and factory.
* Eff:
* VP: 12
* **Delta: Busts the site removing claim and factory, no endgame**

### Pan Sapiens Future
* Req: Have 3 Factories connected to Space Elevators.  Colonist must be at a
  factory connected to a space elevator.
* Eff: **Casus belli**
* VP (endgame): +2 VP for each glory chit owned.

### Secession Future
* Req: 2 Promoted Human Colonists at a promoted Anchored Bernal
* Eff: **Casus Belli**
* VP: 10

### Seti Future
* Req: Industrialize 2 jovian trojans, 1 each in the greek & trojan
  camps. Colonist must be at one of the trojans.
* Eff: As a free action perform 1 inspiration + 1 homestead
* VP: 10

### Submariner Future
* Req: Build 3 Submarine Factories or Colonies, colonist at submarine factory
  (or colony)
* Eff: Doubles your dirtside hydration (not cumulative with other modifiers)
* Note: **This does not apply to home bernals**

### Supreme Cult Future
* Req: Active Law in authority. (Module 0)
* Eff: May lobby without removing the delegate used. All Seniority Disks (past
  and future) migrate to authority.
* VP (endgame: must control colonist): 10

### TNO Future
* Req: Industrialize 2 Sites in the Neptune Zone, colonist at Neptune factory.
* Eff: Can homestead as a free action
* VP: 12

### Uplift Future
* Req: Robots not emancipated, Crew/Colonist at a promoted Bernal & spend 20
  Aqua
* Eff: Every Robot becomes emancipated, **Casus belli**
* VP: 12


## 1D5. List of Colonist Futures
a. New Venus Future: Req = Decommission an operational thruster and its
    supports with net thrust 7+ on an Industrialized Synodic Comet of yours. Bust
    the Site and remove its Claim and Factory. Note: No Tokens (e.g. the Factory)
    need to remain on Venus or comet to attain this Future. For thematic effect
    (it has no game effect unless play is continued beyond the endgame), place the
    Venus overlay card. (V8b) Immediately earn 12 VP.
b. Supreme Cult Future: Req = Active Law in authority. Ongoing Effects: May
    lobby without removing the delegate used. All Seniority Disks (past and
    future) migrate to authority. Endgame: If Active Law is in authority you
    earn 10 VP.
c. Artificial Consciousness Future: Req = You have 2 promoted Colonists at an
    Astrobiology Dirtside. Ongoing Effect: May free market any number of
    cards. Immediately earn 10 VP.
d. Seti Future: Req = Industrialize 2 jovian trojans, 1 each in the greek &
    trojan camps. Ongoing Effect: As a free action perform 1 inspiration then
    1 homestead. Immediately earn 10 VP.
e. Secession Future: Req = You have 2 Promoted Human Colonists at a promoted
    Anchored Bernal. Casus belli for War. (1D2c) Immediately earn 7 VP.
f. Footfall Future: Req = Decommission an operational thruster and its
    supports with net thrust 7+ on an Industrialized Synodic Comet of
    yours. Bust the Site and remove its Claim and Factory. Casus belli for
    War. (1D2c) Immediately earn 10 VP.
g. ET Life Future: Req = You have 2 or more Astrobiological Colonies. Endgame:
    You earn +2 VP for each of your Astrobiological Colonies.
h. Aerostat Future: Req = You have a Promoted Bernal with an Atmospheric
    Aerostat Dirtside. Ongoing Effect: Can homestead as a free action. You
    immediately earn 14 VP.
i. Pan Sapiens Future: Req = You have 3 Factories connected to Space
    Elevators. Casus belli for War. (1D2c), Endgame: You earn +2 VP for each
    of your glory chits.
j. Dyson Bubble Future: Req = Both Sites of Mercury industrialized by any
    player. You immediately earn +5 VP per Factory you own on Mercury.
k. Submariner Future: Req = Build 3 Submarine Factories or Colonies. Ongoing
    Effect: Doubles your dirtside hydration (not cumulative with other
    modifiers).
l. Beanstalk Future: Req = Have 3+ Space Elevators built by any player. Only 1
    player is allowed to complete a Beanstalk future. Endgame: You earn 3 VP
    for each Factory (of any player) connected to a Space Elevator.
m. TNO Future: Req = Industrialize 2 Sites in the Neptune Zone. Ongoing
    Effect: Can homestead as a free action. You immediately earn 12 VP.
n. Uplift Future: Req = Robots not emancipated. (2A6b) Crew/Colonist at a
    promoted Bernal & spend 20 Aqua. Effect: Every Robot becomes
    emancipated. Casus belli for War. (1D2c) You immediately earn 12 VP.
