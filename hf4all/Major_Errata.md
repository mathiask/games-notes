* auctioneer gets money (unless they won), decides ties when not involved
* afterburner: n *steps* for one thrust, before net weight, *plus* 1 cooling
* non-operational thrusters (e.g. after damage) can still coast
** .. spend bonus burns etc., but not pick up new additional bonus burns
* 1st player permission needed to prospect Luna
* Baton pass changes 1st player
* Anarchy => no faction privelege, Felonious, no active law, purge roll
* Colonization: delegate for colonist ideology (optional)
* Martial law => no baton pass
* Atmospheric sites cannot be raygun prospected from range > 0
* Glitch affects the (glitchable) stack with the most cards
* Mobile factories and Nanofacture
* Space elevators auto-claim
* Home Bernal => 1 aqua per turn
* Epic hazard roles risk the unit attempting it (freighter/human/supports)
* Boost to home bernal for double cost (optional)
* Colonists bernal and contract cards are immune to pad explosions/debris
** Contracts also to budget cuts
** Cards at anchored bernal are also immune to solar flares and pad explosions
* Promoted bernals support two colonists
