Ark Nova Rule Clarifications
============================

Easily Forgotten Rules
----------------------
* In multiplayer: take an X if you initiate a break
* Playing cards from the display costs extra (# on folder)

* Continent bonuses
** Continent icons on partner zoos count
** Multiple continent icons on one animal each reduce the cost with partner zoo

* Building
** Pavilions increase appeal by 1
** Multi-builds must be of different buildings
** Filling the zoo => 7 appeal
*+ Special buildings allow relocating existing animals (when built)
