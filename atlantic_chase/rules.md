Atlantic Chase Rule Clarifications
==================================

* Surprise only triggers if attacking TF is faster.

* Time Lapse: only CV launch site for Air Strike action needs to remain
* IC/VfI as combat result is not immediate but at end of turn, replacing 4forI etc.
* MISS only IC if with intel marker

* Intel Limit: if IL cannot be invoked, remove all but INTEL and without
  creating holes

* Convoy limited actions: no air strike, naval search, engage

* Optional Restriction Rules:
** Damaged Ship Restriction (like convoys)
** CV Limit (6)
** Battles remove Evasive Manoeuver Markers
