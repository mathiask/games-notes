Well... I played this 2p last night. I *think* I have some easy tweaks to the 2p setup that would let this game play solo as a beat-your-high-score game. The following is *completely* untested. There is a really minor 'dummy' player component, who does not score, but randomly takes control of some sectors.

Setup:

Per 2p rules, but with the following changes:
- Mix all 3 colors of neutral subs together (the three colors you aren't playing)
- Choose a 'dummy' player color for bots that will fight you for control of the sectors
- Instead of the 2nd player setup, place 3 octopods, 1 crystal, and 1 neutral submarine in the sector that would be the second player's sector.
- Roll 1d6 twice (reroll duplicates). A=1, B=2...F=6. Place a neutral player's bot in those two sectors.
- Place only your Engineer and Scientist where they belong, the dummy doesn't bother with these.

Gameplay:

Each round, you *always* go first, followed by the dummy. The dummy *always* gets the last turn in any round, after you pass.

On your turn, you play the game as normal.

On the dummy's turn, roll a die. If it's 4+, roll again, and place a dummy's bot on that sector (A=1, B=2...F=6), pushing any bots on the sector into the loading area per the normal rules. The dummy will occasionally replace it's own bots. No biggie.

Round Setup:

After placing items according to the center tile, also roll 1d6 (A=1, B=2...F=6), and place a neutral submarine according to that roll. This follows the normal neutral sub rules -- if impossible, place it in the next legal sector, clockwise from where it was supposed to be placed.

Intermediate Scoring:

Same as the normal game, but you compare sector majority against the dummy player's bots. If the dummy has the majority, you simply don't get the bonus. If you're tied, you get 3 KP.

Final Scoring:

No changes.

Challenge:

Beat your high scores!
