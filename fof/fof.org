* Easily overlooked rules and unexpected consequences
- smoke/incoming on firing card moves VOF to smoke card (6.1.2) =>
  - ammo is still expended
  - need to be commanded to go back to firing at other card
- VOF stays after capture
- upper story LOS, e.g., => VOF on entire card
- face-down cards are assumed to be at level 1
- "A" hit results for units with named "F" side are interpreted as "F"
- grenade attacks ignore weather, smoke, etc.
- Action can only be done *once per impulse*
  - no two infiltrations, also not platoon and non-platoon
  - all activated PLT HQ and CO Staff act in the /same/ impulse
- tripods and mortars
  - cannot infiltrate (no H either)
  - not fire when exposed
  - /tripods/ draw an extra card when concentrating fire
** Less than clear rules
- ^G! grants +2 spotting bonus
- 6.3.4 vs. 6.1.2: fire automtically moves to the closest occupied card
  - not only by movement, as long as there are targets in the same PDF
  - does /not/ apply an P range (P => no PDF): only follows unit
    - P range rated units keep firing on the card
- Units in bunkers/pillboxes do /no/ get free grenade attacks
- AT movement:
  - VOF/PDF placement: after step 4 (taking effect next turn)
  - "Move then AT Fire": Move&AT Fire in Step 3, reaction fire last

* Notes
- grenades: hits stack, misses don't, hits don't stack with artillary or
  other FOV. No free reaction when pinned, LATs only when hit. Hits don't
  disappear when LOS is lost later (unlike concentrate fire)
- Enemy actions: random card, on card pinned/LAT, good, leader
  - bunkers keep PDF, only exit for grenade on card and fall back
- PCs markers: alphabetical order, then random
- LATs are (mostly) green => draw modifiers, in particular for rally
- initiative 1 draw for staff is unmodified
- pinned units can
  - move to /safe/ cards
  - be "v&v" commanded to unpin
- moving/falling back units stay pinned (if pinned) + become exposed

* Tactics
- move away from long range enemy to make it "go away"
